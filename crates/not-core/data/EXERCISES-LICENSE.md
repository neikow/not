# Exercise data

`exercises.json` is derived from the exercise database of [wger](https://wger.de), the free fitness
tracker, fetched with `scripts/fetch-exercises.sh`. Each exercise is licensed by its author under
the licence named in its `license` field — CC-BY-SA 4.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>), CC-BY-SA 3.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>) or CC0 — with the author in its `author` field.

The adaptation (selected fields, English and French names only, compact JSON) is shared under the
same terms. The apps credit wger and each exercise's author where the data is shown.
